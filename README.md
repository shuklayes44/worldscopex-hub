# WorldScopeX Hub

Build a professional news/media website for the brand WorldScopeX. Brand identity: WorldScopeX. Tagline: INDIA • WORLD • GEOPOLITICS • ECONOMY • TECH. Create a premium, original editorial design inspired by the cleanliness and information hierarchy of major publications such as Reuters, BBC, Bloomberg and Mint, but do NOT copy their branding, logos, exact layouts, or proprietary styling.

Goal: This is the first production-ready frontend for a future high-traffic news website that will later connect to an AI research/verification/publishing system. It must be suitable for GitHub-based deployment and future migration/hosting. Prioritize fast loading, responsive mobile-first UX, accessibility, semantic HTML, clean component architecture, SEO-friendly structure, and a polished editorial feel.

Homepage sections: top utility/header area; WorldScopeX logo/wordmark area; primary navigation with Home, India, World, Geopolitics, Economy & Business, Technology, Explainers; a breaking-news ticker; a featured lead story with large image and headline; latest news article grid; trending stories; category sections for India, World, Geopolitics, Economy & Business, Technology, and Explainers; newsletter CTA; footer with About, Contact, Privacy Policy, Disclaimer, Terms, social links and copyright.

Article pages: category, headline, summary/dek, author, published/updated date, hero image, article body, source/context block, related stories, share controls, and recommended articles. Create a reusable article-card system and category-page layout.

Design: modern premium editorial, strong typography hierarchy, generous whitespace, clean grid, subtle borders, restrained accent color, excellent contrast, professional but not boring. Use WorldScopeX branding consistently. No text/logo clutter inside article imagery. Avoid excessive rounded cards or flashy gradients. Make it look like a serious global news publication.

Content: use realistic placeholder/sample stories only, clearly structured so they can later be replaced by real verified articles. Do not present invented current events as factual. Include enough sample content to demonstrate every section.

SEO/readiness: semantic headings, metadata structure, canonical-ready URLs, Open Graph/Twitter-card-ready metadata, sitemap/robots-ready architecture, fast images, responsive design, and dedicated legal/info pages needed for a future AdSense application. Include sensible ad placeholder slots in the layout, clearly labeled as ad spaces, but do not make the page feel ad-heavy.

Important future architecture: keep article data/content separated from presentation so an AI publishing bot or CMS can later populate articles. Do not require a backend for the initial demo. Make the frontend easy to export/sync to GitHub. Do not add authentication or payments at this stage.

Build the complete initial website, not just a landing page. Make navigation and sample article/category pages functional in the preview. Use high-quality royalty-free/placeholder image sources that can later be replaced, and avoid copyright-infringing assets.
